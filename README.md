## Research

I study **GPU scheduling and LLM systems**, focusing on performance prediction, resource management in shared GPU clusters, and the execution of multi-agent LLM workflows.

## Crater

I lead the development of [Crater](https://github.com/raids-lab/crater), an open-source Kubernetes platform for AI training and inference on shared GPU clusters. My work on Crater focuses on platform architecture, resource scheduling, and job management.
